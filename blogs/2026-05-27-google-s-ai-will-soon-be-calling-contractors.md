Google announced at Google I/O on May 20, 2026 that its 'Ask for Me' AI feature, which calls local businesses on a homeowner's behalf to check pricing and availability, is expanding to home repair with a nationwide US rollout this summer. Contractors are advised to optimize their Google Business Profiles and adopt pricing transparency ahead of the rollout.
